## 1. Audit Methodology & Acceptance Rule

In accordance with strict audit instructions:
- **No requirement is marked `VERIFIED` unless its defined acceptance criteria are completely satisfied by verified evidence (source implementation + passing automated regression tests or live acceptance runs).**
- All 44 requirements (REQ-001 through REQ-044) are rigorously tracked with exact file paths, test paths, execution results, and limitations.
- Historical audit claims have been re-evaluated against the active codebase. Obsolete claims (such as lack of SQLite, missing Hugging Face, lack of multimodal vision feedback, or dragon companion references) are resolved and replaced with verified reality.
