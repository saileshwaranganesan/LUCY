## 1. Audit Methodology & Acceptance Rule

In accordance with strict audit instructions:
- **No requirement is marked `Accepted` unless its defined acceptance criteria are completely satisfied by verified evidence.**
- Features verified via unit and automated tests but lacking external service integration or production packaging remain at **`Tested`** or **`Implemented`**.
- Original requirement IDs (REQ-001 through REQ-012) are preserved.
- Missing requirements identified during milestone progress are added explicitly (REQ-013 through REQ-024) to establish full traceability against the product vision.
